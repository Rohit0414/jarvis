🧪 Smart Status Logic

Our AI categorizes every product into one of four "Health States" based on Days of Coverage:

Out/Critical (0-7 Days): You are losing money. Restock immediately.
Healthy (7-60 Days): Optimal stock levels. No action required.
Overstock (60+ Days): Capital is locked. Do not reorder.

🩹 FAQ & Success Requirements

🗿 Why is my suggested buy zero?

The AI only suggests a buy if Forecasted Demand > Current Stock. If you have enough stock to cover the next 30 days, the suggested buy remains zero to protect your cash flow.

📝 Required Sales History

The engine requires "Confirmed" or "Done" Sales Orders. Draft quotations are ignored to prevent noise in the AI model. We recommend at least 6 months of data for high accuracy.

🔑 Where is my License Key?

Sign up at niyulabs.com. Your unique key will be available on your customer dashboard under "My Modules."

🛒 How are vendors selected?

The module automatically uses the first vendor listed in the product's "Purchase" tab. Ensure at least one vendor is configured to enable automated PO generation.
