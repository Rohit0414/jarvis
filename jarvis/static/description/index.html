The Engine

How it works

From your database to the cloud and back. A seamless, secure, and automated loop.

🔍
1. Collect

Odoo aggregates daily sales from confirmed Orders (last 2 years). Data is compressed (gzip) for speed.

☁
2. AI Cloud

Secure HTTPS push to api.niyulabs.com. The AI crunching happens remotely, returning a Job ID.

⏳
3. Poll

A smart cron checks status every minute. It sleeps when idle and wakes only when a job is active.

📥
4. Result

Forecasts, charts, and suggested buys are written to Odoo. Statuses (Critical, Healthy) are updated.

📁 Data: sale_order_line + sale_order 🔒 Security: Single Job Lock ⏰ Daily Cron: ~02:00
📚

Key concepts

The fundamental metrics that drive your forecasting engine.

🤖
AI Demand (30d)

The predicted quantity needed for the next 30 days. Derived from Niyu cloud based on your last 2 years of sales history.

Field: forecast_30d
🛒
Suggested buy

Quantity you should order: max(0, AI Demand − stock). It is zero when you have enough stock to cover the forecast.

📅
Days of coverage

How many days current stock will last at the forecasted daily demand rate. Used to calculate status.

🛡
Coverage status

Stockout (Out), Critical (< 7 days), Healthy (7–60 days), or Overstock (> 60 days). Drives row colors.

⚠
Revenue at Risk

Sum of forecast_30d × price for products with zero stock. The potential money lost if you don’t restock.

💰
Capital Needed

Cash needed to fulfill suggested buys: suggested_buy × cost. Helps you budget your next PO run.

Troubleshooting & FAQ

Get unstuck fast. Browse common issues and solutions below.

🔍 Tip: Use your browser's Find (Ctrl+F) to search keywords.
🗿
"No sales history found"
What do I do?
▼
✓

Cause: The system needs confirmed Sales Orders (state "Sale" or "Done") with order dates in the last 2 years for Storable or Consumable products. Quotations, drafts, and canceled orders are excluded.

How to fix:

  • 1. Go to Sales → Orders and confirm you have orders marked as "Sale" or "Done".
  • 2. Check Inventory → Products. Ensure products in those orders have type Storable or Consumable (not Service).
  • 3. Verify order dates span 2+ years for better AI accuracy.
  • 4. Run Initial Sync again in Inventory → Configuration → Settings.
💡 Pro tip: At least 10–20 order lines per product over 6 months provides solid AI training data.
🔑
"Please enter your Niyu API Key…"
License key not found
▼
✓

Cause: Your Niyu License Key is missing or invalid. Without it, the system can't connect to the forecasting engine.

How to fix:

Step-by-step: Inventory → Configuration → Settings
↓
Scroll to "Niyu AI Configuration"
↓
Paste key in "License Key" field
↓
Click Save
↓
Click Run Initial Sync
🔑 Key from: Sign up or log in at niyulabs.com. Your key is on the dashboard.
⏳
"A forecast is already running"
Sync stuck or waiting
▼
✓

Cause: Only one forecast job can run at a time (concurrency guard). The system is either actively processing or a previous job is stale.

Solutions (in order):

  • 1 Wait 1–2 minutes. Check the Command Center badge; if it changes from "Syncing…" to "Active", the job completed successfully.
  • 2 Refresh the page. The UI may not have updated yet.
  • 3 Check logs. Go to Settings → Technical → Logs to see if there's an error message.
  • 4 Stale job? If "Syncing…" lasts > 15 minutes, the system auto-clears it on the next scheduler run or manual sync.
⏱️ Typical time: Most forecasts complete in 30–60 seconds. If stuck longer, network issues or Niyu cloud delays may be involved.
🛒
No "Create PO" button or cart icon
Cannot create purchase orders
▼
✓

Cause: The "Create PO" button only appears when both conditions are met:

1 Suggested buy > 0 (product needs restocking)
2 Product has ≥1 vendor (Supplier configured)

How to fix:

  • → Open the product form (Inventory → Products).
  • → Go to the Purchase tab.
  • → Add at least one Supplier (vendor). This is the "Product Supplier" relationship.
  • → Click Save. Then run a new forecast sync if needed.
📋 Note: The first vendor in the list will be used as the default supplier for the suggested buy calculation.

How often does the forecast refresh?
When is my data updated?
▼
✓

Answer: By default, a scheduled cron job runs daily at 02:00 UTC (configurable). You can also trigger a manual sync anytime.

⏰ Daily Cron: Runs automatically at 02:00 UTC. Data refreshes overnight.
🔄 Manual Sync: Go to Inventory → Configuration → Settings → "Run Initial Sync" button anytime.

Processing time: Most forecasts complete in 30–60 seconds. Check the Last Sync timestamp in the Command Center dashboard.
