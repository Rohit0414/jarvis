FAQ

Is my data safe?

Yes. Niyu uses a stateless RAM-processing architecture. We process sales velocity in volatile memory and purge it immediately. No transaction data is ever stored.

Multi-Company Support?

Absolutely. You can map Niyu to separate Odoo companies or specific warehouse clusters within a single database.

Keep my old rules?

Yes. Niyu runs in parallel with Odoo's native reordering rules. Use Niyu for predictive growth and Odoo rules as your ultimate safety net.

Which Odoo Versions?

Niyu is verified and optimized for Odoo 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, and 19 (Community and Enterprise editions).
