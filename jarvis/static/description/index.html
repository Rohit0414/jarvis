Technical FAQ

Everything you need to know.

✶ Is my sales data safe?

Yes. Niyu uses a Stateless RAM Architecture. Your sales velocity is processed in volatile memory and purged instantly. No transaction history is ever stored on our servers.

✶ Community or Enterprise?

Both. Niyu is optimized for Odoo 14 through 19. It tracks standard Odoo "Stock Moves," so it works perfectly regardless of your UI or theme.

✶ Does it slow down Odoo?

No. The complex math happens on our GPU Clusters. Your Odoo server only receives the final "Draft Purchase Order" data, keeping your server 100% fast.

✶ Multi-Warehouse Support?

Absolutely. Niyu can forecast demand per specific location or aggregate your entire network for a centralized purchasing plan.
