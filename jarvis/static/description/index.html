View 2: Action Lines

A working queue for planners, not just a forecast table.

This module goes further. It creates Action Lines that turn demand and stock data into practical decisions. Each line tells you the next path: buy, transfer, split, watch, ignore for now, or fix setup first.

Action Lines are where the product becomes useful. They are flexible enough for real warehouse work. A line can tell you: how much to buy, which vendor to buy from, whether another warehouse can cover part of the need, whether a transfer should happen instead of a purchase, or whether the best answer is to split: transfer some, buy some.

✶ This is especially useful in multi-warehouse operations where the right answer is often not “buy everything.”

Multi-warehouse planning
without the usual mess

In many businesses, stock in the wrong place is the problem. One warehouse is short. Another one has extra. The team does not notice in time, so they buy again.

This module checks that. It suggests internal transfers when stock already exists elsewhere.

View 3: Transfer or Split Recommendation

Move stock when it makes sense. Buy only what is still needed.

And when one source is not enough, it can handle split planning: move what you can from internal stock, buy the remaining quantity. That gives a more realistic plan and usually leads to better stock use.

Planning rules and exclusions
are what make it practical

Real companies have exceptions. Not every product is restocked the same way. You need the ability to shape the AI's logic so the generated plan matches how your business actually operates.

View 4: Planning Rules

Define behavior at the SKU & Warehouse level.

Tell the system to respect vendor Minimum Order Quantities (MOQs), enforce specific packaging multiples, add safety stock days, and follow precise warehouse routing. Your business rules dictate the final math, ensuring the system never suggests an order you can't actually place.

View 5: Exclusions

Keep the noise out of your planning queue.

Easily flag products that are being phased out (end-of-life), highly customized make-to-order items, or seasonal goods that don't need automated replenishment right now. Leaving these out guarantees a clean, distraction-free action queue for your purchasing team.

Product Documentation

A quick guide to getting started with Niyu AI Planning.

1. Getting Started

📦

Installation

Install the module like any normal Odoo module. It works with: Odoo Enterprise, Odoo Community, Odoo.sh, On-premise.

⚙

Setup & API Sync

Go to the module settings and enter your API key. This key connects your Odoo instance to our forecasting server. Then run the initial sync.

2. Menu Navigation Guide

Planning Overview

The manager view. Open this first to understand what needs action now and where pressure is building.

Action Lines

The planner view. Each line shows the next practical step: buy, transfer, or split.

Rules & Exclusions

Control how planning behaves. Shape the logic for your business instead of letting every item behave the same way.
