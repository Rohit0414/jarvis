Technical FAQ

Common Questions

Do you store or share my data?

No. We do not keep your business data as a stored customer dataset. It is used temporarily for forecasting and planning processing, then cleared.

Is this only a forecast viewer?

No. It is a planning and replenishment workflow. The main value is turning that forecast into clear actions (Buy, Transfer, Split).

Can it suggest internal transfers?

Yes. That is one of its strongest use cases. It can even suggest split decisions: transfer some and buy the rest.

Do I need a separate tool outside Odoo?

No. That is the point. It gives you a complete planning workflow inside your existing Odoo environment.
