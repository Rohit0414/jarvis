Inventory Forecast

AI-powered demand forecasting and smart reordering for Odoo. One of the flagship solutions by Niyu Labs for inventory planning.

🏁 Quick start

Get up and running in four steps:

1.
Install the module and go to Inventory → Configuration → Settings. In Niyu AI Configuration, enter your Niyu License Key (from niyulabs.com) and click Save.
2.
Click Run Initial Sync. Wait for the success message; results appear in about 1 minute.
3.
Open Inventory → Niyu AI to see the Command Center (KPIs and Replenishment Board).
4.
Click Inventory List to see the full Smart Stock list. Use the cart icon or Create Purchase Order to generate POs from the forecast.

📝 Prerequisites

Before using Inventory Forecast, ensure:

  • ✔ Odoo apps — Stock (Inventory), Sales, and Purchase are installed. The module depends on them.
  • ✔ Sales history — You have confirmed Sales Orders (state Sale or Done) with order date in the last 2 years. Quotations and drafts are not used.
  • ✔ Products — Products you want to forecast are Storable or Consumable (not Service). Set product type in Inventory → Products → product form.
  • ✔ Vendors (for POs) — Products you want to reorder have at least one supplier on the Purchase tab. The first supplier is used for suggested buy and PO creation.
  • ✔ License key — You have a Niyu License Key from niyulabs.com. Enter it in Inventory → Configuration → Settings → Niyu AI Configuration.
  • ✔ Network — Your Odoo server can reach api.niyulabs.com over HTTPS (port 443) for sync and polling.

📊 Overview

Inventory Forecast connects your Odoo sales history to Niyu’s cloud AI to predict demand per product for the next 30 days. It computes suggested order quantities, days of coverage, capital needed to restock, and revenue at risk. You get a Command Center dashboard, a full Smart Stock list with status badges and filters, and one-click purchase order generation grouped by vendor.

⚙ How it works

When you run a forecast (manually from Settings or via the daily cron), Odoo collects daily sales by product from confirmed Sales Orders of the last 2 years for storable and consumable products. This data is compressed (gzip) and sent to api.niyulabs.com with your license key. The cloud returns a job ID; a scheduled action polls every 1 minute until the job is done. Results are then written into niyu.forecast.result: forecast quantity, chart JSON, and last sync time. Suggested buy is computed as max(0, forecast_30d − current_stock). Days of coverage and status (Critical / Overstock / Healthy / Stockout) are computed from current stock and the 30-day demand.

Data source: sale_order_line + sale_order Concurrent syncs: Single job lock Polling cron: Every 1 min Daily cron: Once per day
🔃 What gets synced
Sent to Niyu cloud (request)

Odoo builds a list of objects: { "product_id": <id>, "date": "YYYY-MM-DD", "qty": <float> } for each (product, day) from confirmed SOs in the last 2 years. The list is JSON-encoded, gzip-compressed, and POSTed to api.niyulabs.com/forecast with headers X-License-Key, Content-Encoding: gzip, Content-Type: application/json. Response (200): { "job_id": "<string>" }.

Received from cloud (poll result)

Polling GETs api.niyulabs.com/result/<job_id> with X-License-Key. When status is "done", data is a list of objects per product: product_id, forecast_qty, chart_data (JSON string). Odoo writes these into niyu.forecast.result (create or update by product_id), sets last_updated, last_sync_time, and last_sync_status.

📚 Key concepts

AI Demand (30d) / forecast_30d The predicted quantity of a product that will be sold (or needed) in the next 30 days. Comes from Niyu cloud based on your last 2 years of sales.
Suggested buy Quantity you should order: max(0, AI Demand 30d − current stock). Zero when you have enough stock to cover the forecast.
Days of coverage / Days left How many days current stock will last at the forecasted daily demand (forecast_30d ÷ 30). Used to derive status.
Coverage status Stockout (out of stock), Critical (< 7 days), Healthy (7–60 days), or Overstock (> 60 days). Drives row colors and filters.
Revenue at Risk Sum of (forecast_30d × list_price) for products with zero stock. Potential revenue lost if you don’t restock.
Restock Capital / Capital Needed Money needed to fulfill suggested buys: suggested_buy × standard_price per product; Restock Capital is the total across all items needing purchase.
Last AI Sync When the last forecast run completed and results were written to Odoo. Shown in the Command Center header.

👣 Workflow (step-by-step)

1
Trigger — You click Run Initial Sync in Settings, or the daily cron runs (e.g. 02:00).
2
Data collection — Odoo reads confirmed Sales Orders (last 2 years), aggregates daily sales per product (storable/consumable only).
3
Send to cloud — Payload (product_id, date, qty) is compressed (gzip) and POSTed to api.niyulabs.com with your license key. Cloud returns a job_id.
4
Polling — The scheduled action Niyu AI: Poll Forecast Results runs every minute. It GETs the result for job_id until status is "done" (typically ~1 minute).
5
Results in Odoo — For each product, forecast_30d and chart_data are written (create or update) in niyu.forecast.result. last_sync_time and last_sync_status are updated.
6
You see data — Command Center and Smart Stock list show updated KPIs, suggested buy, status, and Replenishment Board. Open Inventory → Niyu AI or Inventory List.
7
Create POs — From the list (cart icon or multi-select + Action → Create Purchase Orders) or from a product form (Create Purchase Order). POs are grouped by vendor with origin "Niyu Smart Stock".

🛒 Creating purchase orders

Purchase orders are created from forecast lines that have suggested_buy > 0 and a vendor (first supplier on the product). Lines are grouped by vendor; one PO per vendor with origin Niyu Smart Stock. Each order line uses the product, suggested_buy as quantity, and the vendor’s price from the product’s first seller.

From the list: Use the cart button on a row to create POs for that single record (same logic: one vendor, one PO). You can also select multiple rows and run the server action Create Purchase Orders (binding on niyu.forecast.result) to create POs for all selected lines grouped by vendor.
From the form: Click Create Purchase Order in the header to create a PO for that product only (if it has suggested_buy > 0 and a vendor). After creation, the action opens the list of generated POs (purchase.order) in list and form view.

💡 How to use

First-time setup
  1. Go to Inventory → Configuration → Settings.
  2. Scroll to Niyu AI Configuration.
  3. Enter your Niyu License Key (get it at niyulabs.com) and click Save.
  4. Click Run Initial Sync. Wait for the success message; results appear in about 1 minute.
  5. Open Inventory → Niyu AI to see the Command Center and replenishment data.
Daily workflow
  1. Open Inventory → Niyu AI (Command Center). Check Last AI Sync and the three KPI cards (Revenue at Risk, Critical Items, Restock Capital).
  2. Use the Replenishment Board to see top items. Toggle Critical Only to focus on items with < 7 days coverage or stockout.
  3. Click Inventory List to open the full Smart Stock list (default filter: Need Purchase).
  4. To create POs: select one or more rows and click the cart icon on a row, or use the Action menu → Create Purchase Orders for selected lines. Review the generated POs and confirm as needed.
Viewing a product forecast
  1. From the Command Center, click Review on a row to open that product’s forecast form.
  2. Or from the Smart Stock list, click a product row to open the form.
  3. On the form you see Current stock, AI Demand (30d), Suggested buy, Status, Days of coverage, and Last updated. Open the AI Prediction Graph tab to see the chart.
  4. If the product has suggested_buy > 0, use Create Purchase Order in the header to generate a PO for that product.
Running a new forecast
  1. Go to Inventory → Configuration → Settings → Niyu AI Configuration.
  2. Click Run Initial Sync. If a forecast is already running, you’ll see a warning; wait a moment and try again. Results refresh in about 1 minute.
  3. Optional: use Automation → Scheduled Actions to adjust Niyu AI: Daily Demand Forecast (e.g. time of day) so forecasts run automatically every day.
Using filters on the Smart Stock list
  • Critical Risk — Only items with status Critical or Stockout (< 7 days or out of stock).
  • Need Purchase — Only items with Suggested buy > 0 (default when opening the list).
  • Healthy — Only items with Suggested buy = 0 (no reorder needed).
  • Use the search bar to find a product by name. Use Group by Product if you want to group results.

Tips & best practices

  • • Keep at least one vendor (supplier) on every storable product you want to reorder; otherwise Suggested buy won’t create a PO.
  • • Run a sync after big sales events or season changes so the 30-day forecast reflects recent demand.
  • • Start with the Critical Risk or Critical Only view to tackle stockouts and < 7 days coverage first.
  • • Use Capital Needed (optional column in Smart Stock list) to plan budget; use the list footer sum for total restock capital.
  • • Review generated POs before confirming: quantities come from Suggested buy; you can adjust lines or discard a PO if needed.
  • • If you don’t want daily automatic syncs, disable or delete the scheduled action Niyu AI: Daily Demand Forecast and run Run Initial Sync manually when you want a refresh.

🧠 Coverage status logic

Status is computed from current stock, forecast_30d, and derived daily demand (forecast_30d / 30).

  • Stockout — current_stock ≤ 0 and forecast_30d > 0. Days of coverage = 0.
  • Critical — days_of_coverage < 7 (and not stockout).
  • Overstock (Warning) — days_of_coverage > 60.
  • Healthy — otherwise (7 ≤ days ≤ 60).
  • If forecast_30d = 0 and current_stock > 0, days_of_coverage is set to 999 (treated as "Safe" in the dashboard).
BadgeMeaning
StockoutOut of stock; demand > 0. Reorder ASAP.
CriticalLess than 7 days stock. Prioritize.
Overstock> 60 days coverage. Reduce orders.
Healthy7–60 days coverage. No action.

🩹 Troubleshooting & FAQ

"No sales history found"

You need confirmed Sales Orders (state Sale or Done) with order date in the last 2 years, for Storable or Consumable products. Quotations and draft/canceled orders are ignored. Check that products have type Storable or Consumable (Inventory app → Products).

"Please enter your Niyu API Key..."

Go to Inventory → Configuration → Settings, scroll to Niyu AI Configuration, enter your license key from niyulabs.com, and Save. Then run Run Initial Sync.

"A forecast is already running"

Only one forecast job runs at a time. Wait about 1 minute for the current job to finish; the status badge will change from Syncing... to Active. If it stays on Syncing... for more than 15 minutes, the job may be stale and will be cleared on the next run.

No Create PO button or cart icon

Suggested buy must be > 0 and the product must have at least one vendor (Supplier). Add a vendor on the product form (Purchase tab) and run a sync if needed.
