Inventory Forecast

AI-powered demand forecasting and smart reordering for Odoo. One of the flagship solutions by Niyu Labs for inventory planning.

Quick start

Get up and running in four steps:

  1. Install the module and go to Inventory → Configuration → Settings. In Niyu AI Configuration, enter your Niyu License Key (from niyulabs.com) and click Save.
  2. Click Run Initial Sync. Wait for the success message; results appear in about 1 minute.
  3. Open Inventory → Niyu AI to see the Command Center (KPIs and Replenishment Board).
  4. Click Inventory List to see the full Smart Stock list. Use the cart icon or Create Purchase Order to generate POs from the forecast.

Prerequisites

Before using Inventory Forecast, ensure:

  • Odoo apps — Stock (Inventory), Sales, and Purchase are installed. The module depends on them.
  • Sales history — You have confirmed Sales Orders (state Sale or Done) with order date in the last 2 years. Quotations and drafts are not used.
  • Products — Products you want to forecast are Storable or Consumable (not Service). Set product type in Inventory → Products → product form.
  • Vendors (for POs) — Products you want to reorder have at least one supplier on the Purchase tab. The first supplier is used for suggested buy and PO creation.
  • License key — You have a Niyu License Key from niyulabs.com. Enter it in Inventory → Configuration → Settings → Niyu AI Configuration.
  • Network — Your Odoo server can reach api.niyulabs.com over HTTPS (port 443) for sync and polling.

Overview

Inventory Forecast connects your Odoo sales history to Niyu’s cloud AI to predict demand per product for the next 30 days. It computes suggested order quantities, days of coverage, capital needed to restock, and revenue at risk. You get a Command Center dashboard, a full Smart Stock list with status badges and filters, and one-click purchase order generation grouped by vendor.

Benefits

Why use Inventory Forecast:

  • Reduce stockouts — AI predicts demand so you reorder before running out; focus on Critical and Stockout items first.
  • Optimize capital — See Restock Capital and per-product cost to restock; order what you need instead of overbuying.
  • Save time — One dashboard (Command Center) and one list (Smart Stock) instead of manual spreadsheets; one-click POs grouped by vendor.
  • Revenue at Risk visibility — Know how much potential revenue is lost when products are out of stock.
  • Data-driven reordering — Suggested buy is based on 2 years of sales and current stock, not guesswork.

Features

Inventory Forecast includes the following capabilities:

  • AI 30-day demand — Per-product forecast for the next 30 days from your last 2 years of confirmed sales, processed by Niyu cloud.
  • Command Center — Dashboard under Inventory → Niyu AI with Revenue at Risk, Critical Items, Restock Capital, and Last AI Sync status.
  • Replenishment Board — Top 50 items needing reorder with 30-day trend sparkline, stockout date, lead time, suggested buy, and Review action.
  • Smart Stock list — Full forecast list with Product, Status badge (Critical / Overstock / Healthy / Stockout), Current Qty, Days Coverage, AI Demand, Suggested Buy, Capital Needed, and per-row Create PO button.
  • Filters — Critical Risk, Need Purchase, Healthy; optional Group by Product.
  • One-click purchase orders — From list (single row or multi-select + action) or from product form; POs grouped by vendor with origin "Niyu Smart Stock".
  • AI Prediction Graph — Form tab with chart from Niyu cloud (time series / sparkline data).
  • Daily automatic sync — Optional scheduled action (e.g. 02:00) to run a new forecast every day.
  • Background processing — Forecast runs in the cloud; polling cron checks every minute until results are ready (~1 minute).
  • Concurrent-sync guard — Only one forecast job at a time; stale jobs (> 15 min) are cleared automatically.

Use cases

Inventory Forecast fits well when:

  • Retail or e‑commerce — You sell many SKUs and need to know what to reorder and how much, without manual analysis.
  • Wholesale / distribution — You manage multiple vendors; the module groups POs by vendor and uses the first supplier per product.
  • Seasonal or variable demand — AI uses 2 years of history to capture trends and seasonality for the 30-day forecast.
  • Stock and purchase already in Odoo — You use Stock, Sales, and Purchase; products have vendors and confirmed SOs so forecasts and POs are actionable.
  • Daily or weekly replenishment — Run the forecast daily (cron) or on demand; check Command Center and create POs from the Smart Stock list.

How it works

When you run a forecast (manually from Settings or via the daily cron), Odoo collects daily sales by product from confirmed Sales Orders of the last 2 years for storable and consumable products. This data is compressed (gzip) and sent to api.niyulabs.com with your license key. The cloud returns a job ID; a scheduled action polls every 1 minute until the job is done. Results are then written into niyu.forecast.result: forecast quantity, chart JSON, and last sync time. Suggested buy is computed as max(0, forecast_30d − current_stock). Days of coverage and status (Critical / Overstock / Healthy / Stockout) are computed from current stock and the 30-day demand.

  • Data source: sale_order_line + sale_order (state in sale/done, date_order last 2 years), product type product/consu.
  • Concurrent syncs: only one forecast job at a time; if a job is older than 15 minutes it is cleared and a new one can start.
  • Polling cron: Niyu AI: Poll Forecast Results runs every 1 minute when active; it is woken when a job is started and can be put to sleep when no jobs remain.
  • Daily cron: Niyu AI: Daily Demand Forecast runs once per day (e.g. 02:00) to start a new forecast automatically.

What gets synced

Sent to Niyu cloud (request)

Odoo builds a list of objects: { "product_id": <id>, "date": "YYYY-MM-DD", "qty": <float> } for each (product, day) from confirmed SOs in the last 2 years. The list is JSON-encoded, gzip-compressed, and POSTed to api.niyulabs.com/forecast with headers X-License-Key, Content-Encoding: gzip, Content-Type: application/json. Response (200): { "job_id": "<string>" }.

Received from cloud (poll result)

Polling GETs api.niyulabs.com/result/<job_id> with X-License-Key. When status is "done", data is a list of objects per product: product_id, forecast_qty, chart_data (JSON string). Odoo writes these into niyu.forecast.result (create or update by product_id), sets last_updated, last_sync_time, and last_sync_status.

Key concepts

Core terms used throughout the module:

  • AI Demand (30d) / forecast_30d — The predicted quantity of a product that will be sold (or needed) in the next 30 days. Comes from Niyu cloud based on your last 2 years of sales.
  • Suggested buy — Quantity you should order: max(0, AI Demand 30d − current stock). Zero when you have enough stock to cover the forecast.
  • Days of coverage / Days left — How many days current stock will last at the forecasted daily demand (forecast_30d ÷ 30). Used to derive status.
  • Coverage status — Stockout (out of stock), Critical (< 7 days), Healthy (7–60 days), or Overstock (> 60 days). Drives row colors and filters.
  • Revenue at Risk — Sum of (forecast_30d × list_price) for products with zero stock. Potential revenue lost if you don’t restock.
  • Restock Capital / Capital Needed — Money needed to fulfill suggested buys: suggested_buy × standard_price per product; Restock Capital is the total across all items needing purchase.
  • Last AI Sync — When the last forecast run completed and results were written to Odoo. Shown in the Command Center header.

Workflow (step-by-step)

End-to-end flow from sync to purchase:

  1. Trigger — You click Run Initial Sync in Settings, or the daily cron runs (e.g. 02:00).
  2. Data collection — Odoo reads confirmed Sales Orders (last 2 years), aggregates daily sales per product (storable/consumable only).
  3. Send to cloud — Payload (product_id, date, qty) is compressed (gzip) and POSTed to api.niyulabs.com with your license key. Cloud returns a job_id.
  4. Polling — The scheduled action Niyu AI: Poll Forecast Results runs every minute. It GETs the result for job_id until status is "done" (typically ~1 minute).
  5. Results in Odoo — For each product, forecast_30d and chart_data are written (create or update) in niyu.forecast.result. last_sync_time and last_sync_status are updated.
  6. You see data — Command Center and Smart Stock list show updated KPIs, suggested buy, status, and Replenishment Board. Open Inventory → Niyu AI or Inventory List.
  7. Create POs — From the list (cart icon or multi-select + Action → Create Purchase Orders) or from a product form (Create Purchase Order). POs are grouped by vendor with origin "Niyu Smart Stock".

Creating purchase orders

Purchase orders are created from forecast lines that have suggested_buy > 0 and a vendor (first supplier on the product). Lines are grouped by vendor; one PO per vendor with origin Niyu Smart Stock. Each order line uses the product, suggested_buy as quantity, and the vendor’s price from the product’s first seller.

From the list: Use the cart button on a row to create POs for that single record (same logic: one vendor, one PO). You can also select multiple rows and run the server action Create Purchase Orders (binding on niyu.forecast.result) to create POs for all selected lines grouped by vendor.

From the form: Click Create Purchase Order in the header to create a PO for that product only (if it has suggested_buy > 0 and a vendor). After creation, the action opens the list of generated POs (purchase.order) in list and form view.

How to use

First-time setup

  1. Go to Inventory → Configuration → Settings.
  2. Scroll to Niyu AI Configuration.
  3. Enter your Niyu License Key (get it at niyulabs.com) and click Save.
  4. Click Run Initial Sync. Wait for the success message; results appear in about 1 minute.
  5. Open Inventory → Niyu AI to see the Command Center and replenishment data.

Daily workflow

  1. Open Inventory → Niyu AI (Command Center). Check Last AI Sync and the three KPI cards (Revenue at Risk, Critical Items, Restock Capital).
  2. Use the Replenishment Board to see top items. Toggle Critical Only to focus on items with < 7 days coverage or stockout.
  3. Click Inventory List to open the full Smart Stock list (default filter: Need Purchase).
  4. To create POs: select one or more rows and click the cart icon on a row, or use the Action menu → Create Purchase Orders for selected lines. Review the generated POs and confirm as needed.

Viewing a product forecast

  1. From the Command Center, click Review on a row to open that product’s forecast form.
  2. Or from the Smart Stock list, click a product row to open the form.
  3. On the form you see Current stock, AI Demand (30d), Suggested buy, Status, Days of coverage, and Last updated. Open the AI Prediction Graph tab to see the chart.
  4. If the product has suggested_buy > 0, use Create Purchase Order in the header to generate a PO for that product.

Running a new forecast

  1. Go to Inventory → Configuration → Settings → Niyu AI Configuration.
  2. Click Run Initial Sync. If a forecast is already running, you’ll see a warning; wait a moment and try again. Results refresh in about 1 minute.
  3. Optional: use Automation → Scheduled Actions to adjust Niyu AI: Daily Demand Forecast (e.g. time of day) so forecasts run automatically every day.

Using filters on the Smart Stock list

  • Critical Risk — Only items with status Critical or Stockout (< 7 days or out of stock).
  • Need Purchase — Only items with Suggested buy > 0 (default when opening the list).
  • Healthy — Only items with Suggested buy = 0 (no reorder needed).
  • Use the search bar to find a product by name. Use Group by Product if you want to group results.

Tips & best practices

  • Keep at least one vendor (supplier) on every storable product you want to reorder; otherwise Suggested buy won’t create a PO.
  • Run a sync after big sales events or season changes so the 30-day forecast reflects recent demand.
  • Start with the Critical Risk or Critical Only view to tackle stockouts and < 7 days coverage first.
  • Use Capital Needed (optional column in Smart Stock list) to plan budget; use the list footer sum for total restock capital.
  • Review generated POs before confirming: quantities come from Suggested buy; you can adjust lines or discard a PO if needed.
  • If you don’t want daily automatic syncs, disable or delete the scheduled action Niyu AI: Daily Demand Forecast and run Run Initial Sync manually when you want a refresh.

Coverage status logic

Status is computed from current stock, forecast_30d, and derived daily demand (forecast_30d / 30).

  • Stockout — current_stock ≤ 0 and forecast_30d > 0. Days of coverage = 0.
  • Critical — days_of_coverage < 7 (and not stockout).
  • Overstock (Warning) — days_of_coverage > 60.
  • Healthy — otherwise (7 ≤ days ≤ 60).
  • If forecast_30d = 0 and current_stock > 0, days_of_coverage is set to 999 (treated as "Safe" in the dashboard).

Troubleshooting & FAQ

"No sales history found"

You need confirmed Sales Orders (state Sale or Done) with order date in the last 2 years, for Storable or Consumable products. Quotations and draft/canceled orders are ignored. Check that products have type Storable or Consumable (Inventory app → Products).

"Please enter your Niyu API Key in Settings > Inventory"

Go to Inventory → Configuration → Settings, scroll to Niyu AI Configuration, enter your license key from niyulabs.com, and Save. Then run Run Initial Sync.

"A forecast is already running"

Only one forecast job runs at a time. Wait about 1 minute for the current job to finish; the status badge will change from Syncing... to Active. If it stays on Syncing... for more than 15 minutes, the job may be stale and will be cleared on the next run so you can start a new one.

No Create PO button or cart icon

Suggested buy must be > 0 and the product must have at least one vendor (Supplier). Add a vendor on the product form (Purchase tab) and run a sync if needed.

Sync status shows Error

Hover the Error badge for the message (e.g. invalid key, rate limit, cloud error). Check your license key, ensure you’re not over rate limits, and try Run Initial Sync again. If the problem persists, contact Niyu Labs support.

When do results appear after Run Initial Sync?

Usually within about 1 minute. The polling cron runs every minute. Refresh the Command Center or Smart Stock list to see updated data and Last AI Sync time.

Scheduled actions

Niyu AI: Poll Forecast Results — Model niyu.polling.job, method cron_check_results. Interval: every 1 minute. Default: inactive (sleeping). Activated when you start a forecast; when there are no more jobs, it can be deactivated again. For each job, it GETs api.niyulabs.com/result/{job_id} with the license key. When status is "done", it writes forecast_30d and chart_data to niyu.forecast.result (create or update by product_id), sets last_sync_time and last_sync_status to success, then deletes the job. Jobs older than 30 minutes are deleted.

Niyu AI: Daily Demand Forecast — Model niyu.sync.engine, method action_start_forecast. Interval: 1 day. Default: active. Next run typically at 02:00. Starts a new forecast job (same as Run Initial Sync); if no license key is set, it exits without error.

Data requirements

  • Confirmed Sales Orders (state sale or done) with date_order within the last 2 years.
  • Products must be Storable or Consumable (product or consu in product.template type/detailed_type).
  • For suggested buy and PO generation, products should have at least one vendor (seller); the first supplier and its delay/price are used.
  • If no sales history is found when starting a forecast, a UserError is raised with a clear message to check the above.

Limitations

Things to keep in mind:

  • First vendor only — Suggested buy and PO creation use the product’s first supplier (first row in the Purchase tab). Multiple suppliers per product are not split across POs.
  • 2 years of sales — Only confirmed SOs with order date in the last 2 years are used. Older history is ignored.
  • Replenishment Board cap — The Command Center table shows up to 50 items (those with suggested_buy > 0, ordered by days of coverage then suggested buy). Use the full Smart Stock list for all products.
  • Storable and consumable only — Service products and other types are excluded from the sync query.
  • One forecast job at a time — A new sync cannot start until the current job finishes or is cleared (e.g. after 15 minutes).
  • No lead-time adjustment in suggested buy — Suggested buy is demand minus current stock; it does not add extra buffer for vendor lead time (you can add safety stock or adjust POs manually).

Integration with Odoo

Inventory Forecast uses standard Odoo apps as follows:

  • Inventory (Stock) — Current stock comes from product.qty_available. Products must be Storable or Consumable. The module adds the Niyu AI menu under the main Inventory menu.
  • Sales — Forecast input is built from confirmed Sales Orders (sale/done) and their lines: product, date, quantity. Only last 2 years of order dates are used.
  • Purchase — Created POs are standard purchase.order records. Vendor, quantity (suggested_buy), and unit price come from the product’s first supplier. Origin is set to "Niyu Smart Stock" so you can filter or report on them.
  • Web — Command Center and the niyu_chart widget use Odoo’s backend assets (OWL / JS).

Data & privacy

What is sent to Niyu cloud: aggregated sales data only — product ID, date (day), and quantity sold per day. No customer names, order IDs, or other PII. Data is compressed (gzip) and sent over HTTPS to api.niyulabs.com with your license key in the request header. The cloud returns forecast quantities and chart data per product; these are stored in Odoo in niyu.forecast.result. Your license key is stored in Odoo system parameters (niyu.license_key). For full terms and data handling, see niyulabs.com.

Reporting & exports

How to use the data for reporting:

  • Smart Stock list — Use Odoo’s list view options: show/hide columns (e.g. Capital Needed, Days Coverage), apply filters (Critical Risk, Need Purchase, Healthy), then export to Excel/CSV via the list view action menu for your own analysis or reports.
  • Purchase orders from Niyu — All POs created by the module have origin Niyu Smart Stock. In Purchase, filter or group by Origin to see only AI-generated orders, or build reports (e.g. total value, by vendor) for restock analytics.
  • Command Center KPIs — Revenue at Risk, Critical Items count, and Restock Capital are computed live from niyu.forecast.result; they reflect the last sync. Use them in daily standups or dashboards; for historical tracking, export the Smart Stock list after each sync or use Odoo reporting on purchase orders.

  • License key storage — The key is stored in ir.config_parameter (niyu.license_key). Protect access to technical parameters if you need to limit who can see the key.

Performance & scale

Considerations for large catalogs and high volume:

  • Sync payload — Sales data is aggregated by (product, day) over 2 years. More products and more orders mean a larger payload; gzip keeps it smaller. The cloud may take slightly longer for very large datasets; results typically appear within 1–2 minutes.
  • Replenishment Board — Only the top 50 items (by days of coverage then suggested buy) are loaded on the Command Center. The full Smart Stock list loads all forecast records; use filters (Critical Risk, Need Purchase) to reduce visible rows and improve list performance.
  • Polling cron — Runs every 1 minute only when a job exists. It does a single GET per job; impact on the server is low.
  • Daily cron — One forecast per day. If you need more frequent syncs, you can create a custom scheduled action that calls the same sync logic; respect Niyu’s rate limits.

Frequently asked questions

Can I run the forecast more than once a day?

Yes. Use Run Initial Sync in Settings whenever you want. Only one job runs at a time; wait for the current one to finish (~1 minute) before starting another. Respect Niyu’s rate limits if you run very frequently.

Can I change the suggested quantity before creating the PO?

The module creates POs with the suggested_buy quantity. After the PO is created, you can edit the order lines in the purchase order form (quantity, price) before confirming. The forecast list is not updated when you change the PO.

Why does a product not appear in the forecast list?

Forecast records are created or updated only for products that have sales history in the last 2 years (confirmed SOs) and are Storable or Consumable. If a product has no sales in that period, the cloud may not return it; new products will appear after they have sold and you run a sync.

Can I use a different vendor than the first supplier?

PO creation uses the first supplier (first row on the product’s Purchase tab). To use another vendor, reorder the suppliers on the product so the desired vendor is first, or create the PO manually from the forecast data.

Does the module work with multi-warehouse or multi-company?

Current stock is from product.qty_available (company-wide available quantity). The module does not split by warehouse or company in the forecast or PO creation. Use standard Odoo multi-warehouse rules for stock by location.

Technical reference (developers)

For developers extending or debugging the module:

  • Models — niyu.forecast.result (forecast records), niyu.sync.engine (abstract, sync logic), niyu.polling.job (job_id, start_time for polling).
  • Sync entry point — niyu.sync.engine action_start_forecast(). Call with from_cron=True in context for silent skip when no license key.
  • Polling — niyu.polling.job cron_check_results(). Writes to niyu.forecast.result and sets niyu.last_sync_time, niyu.last_sync_status, niyu.last_sync_msg.
  • Dashboard data — niyu.forecast.result get_dashboard_stats() returns critical count, capital, revenue_risk, last_sync, sync_status, sync_msg, top_items (with sparkline from chart_data).
  • API base — https://api.niyulabs.com. Endpoints: POST /forecast, GET /result/<job_id>. Header: X-License-Key.
