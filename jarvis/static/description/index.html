It tells you what to do, not just what happened

A lot of tools stop at forecasting. That sounds good until the user still has to answer: “Okay, now what?”

Screenshot 2: Action Lines

Caption: A working queue for planners, not just a forecast table.

This module goes further. It creates Action Lines that turn demand and stock data into practical decisions. Each line tells you the next path: buy, transfer, split, watch, ignore for now, or fix setup first.

This matters because planning is not only about seeing shortage. It is about choosing the right response.

Action Lines are where the product becomes useful. They are flexible enough for real warehouse work. A line can tell you: how much to buy, which vendor to buy from, whether another warehouse can cover part of the need, whether a transfer should happen instead of a purchase, or whether the best answer is split: transfer some, buy some.

✶ This is especially useful in multi-warehouse operations where the right answer is often not “buy everything.”

Multi-warehouse planning without the usual mess

In many businesses, stock is not the problem. Stock in the wrong place is the problem. One warehouse is short. Another one has extra. The team does not notice in time, so they buy again.

This module checks that. It suggests internal transfers when stock already exists elsewhere.

Screenshot 3: Transfer or Split Recommendation

Caption: Move stock when it makes sense. Buy only what is still needed.

And when one source is not enough, it can handle split planning: move what you can from internal stock, buy the remaining quantity. That gives a more realistic plan and usually leads to better stock use.

Planning rules and exclusions are what make it practical

Forecasting alone is not enough. Real companies have exceptions. Some products should behave differently. Some warehouses need different coverage. Some items should be left out completely.

That balance is important: strong enough for serious planning, simple enough to use daily.

Product Manual

📦 Installation

Install the module like any normal Odoo module. It works with: Odoo Enterprise, Odoo Community, Odoo.sh, On-premise.

⚙ Setup

Go to the module settings and enter your API key. This key connects your Odoo instance to our forecasting and planning server. Then run the initial sync. Once the sync is complete, the module starts preparing your planning data and action views.

What each menu means in practical words

Planning Overview

This is the main control panel. Open this first. Use it to understand: what needs action now, stock pressure, buying needed, internal movement, setup blockers. This is the manager view.

Action Lines

This is the working queue. Use this when you are ready to act. Each line shows what the next practical step should be. This is the planner view.

Rules & Exclusions

Control how planning should behave. Use exclusions when certain products or categories should stay out of automated planning to keep the output clean.

Typical use cases

Scenario 1: I want to know what to buy today

Open Planning Overview. Look at urgent action lines. Open Buy Now. Review the quantities and suggested vendors. Create or continue the buying process.

Scenario 2: One warehouse is short but another has stock

Open Planning Overview. Check Internal Rebalance or Warehouse Pressure. Open the suggested lines. Review transfer suggestions. Move stock internally before buying again.

Scenario 3: Split transfer and purchase

Open the relevant action line. Review the split suggestion. Transfer what is available internally. Buy what is still needed.
