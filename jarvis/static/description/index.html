FAQ

Is my data safe?

Yes. We use a stateless architecture. We process sales velocity in memory and purge it immediately. No data is retained on our side.

Does it work with Multi-Company?

Yes. You can configure Niyu for separate companies or separate warehouse clusters within a single Odoo instance.

Can I keep my old rules?

Yes. Niyu runs alongside Odoo's rules. Use Niyu for proactive planning and Odoo rules as a safety net.

What versions of Odoo?

Fully compatible with Odoo 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, and 19 (Community & Enterprise).
