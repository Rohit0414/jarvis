Practical filters

Use Odoo domains to send the right records to Power BI.

Do not dump everything. Build reporting datasets around business rules your team already understands.

Posted invoices only

Useful for finance dashboards where draft invoices should not affect revenue numbers.

[('state','=','posted')]

Confirmed sales only

Keep quotations out of your confirmed sales dashboard.

[('state','in',['sale','done'])]

Active products only

Avoid archived products in inventory and purchase dashboards.

[('active','=',True)]
Realtime ready

Incremental sync for reporting that stays fresh.

For large Odoo databases, full export every time is expensive and slow. Incremental sync exports new and changed records based on safe cursors, so your Power BI refresh has a cleaner source.

Useful for sales orders, invoices, stock moves, products, contacts, purchase orders and other high-volume Odoo tables.
