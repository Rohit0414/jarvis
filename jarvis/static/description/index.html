How to use

The setup is made for business users. You do not need to search and add every model manually.

1

Open Settings

Enable the MCP server, choose role-based setup, and review the generated endpoint.

2

Create Bundles

Pick app groups like Sales, CRM, Inventory, Purchase, Website, or Project. Matching models are selected for you.

3

Assign Users

Assign the bundle to the users who should access that business area.

4

Copy and Connect

Copy the endpoint or token from settings and paste it into ChatGPT, Claude, or your MCP client.
