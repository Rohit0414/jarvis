Technical FAQ

Everything you need to know.

✶ Is my sales data safe?

Yes. Niyu uses a Stateless RAM Architecture. Your sales velocity is processed in volatile memory and purged instantly. No transaction history is ever stored.

✶ Does it slow down Odoo?

No. The complex math happens on our GPU Clusters. Your Odoo server only receives the final "Draft Purchase Order" data.
