The Workflow

How Niyu Works.

01

Ingest & Cleanse

Niyu connects to your Odoo database and pulls 24 months of Stock Moves. Crucially, it cleans the data. It removes "Outliers" (random huge B2B orders) so they don't skew your average daily sales.

  • Removes B2B Bulk Spikes
  • Normalizes "Out of Stock" days
02

Neural Processing

The data is processed by our Neural Engine. We calculate Seasonality Indices for every SKU. We know that sunscreen sells in July and umbrellas sell in November.

GPU Acceleration: Math is done on our cloud GPUs, keeping your Odoo server fast.
03

Auto-Draft POs

Niyu writes the results back to Odoo as Draft Requests for Quotation (RFQs). It groups items by Vendor, fills in the optimal quantity, and sets the "Order Deadline" based on lead times.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe?

Yes. Niyu uses a "Stateless" architecture. We process your sales velocity in volatile RAM memory and purge it immediately after the calculation is sent back to Odoo.

Does it support Multi-Company?

Absolutely. You can map Niyu to separate Odoo companies or specific warehouse clusters within a single database.

Can I keep my old rules?

Yes. Niyu runs in parallel with Odoo's native reordering rules. Use Niyu for predictive growth and Odoo rules as your safety net.

Which Odoo Versions?

Niyu is verified and optimized for Odoo 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, and 19 (Community and Enterprise editions).
