✔ Preparation Checklist

Ensure your Odoo instance is ready for the Niyu AI brain.

📝 Data Quality
  • Confirmed Orders: AI only reads Sales Orders in "Sale" or "Done" state.
  • Storable Products: Only Storable or Consumable products are forecasted (no Services).
  • Date Range: At least 6 months of sales history is recommended for accuracy.
🔒 Connectivity
  • License Key: Valid active key from niyulabs.com.
  • Outbound Access: Your Odoo server must reach api.niyulabs.com via HTTPS.
  • Vendor Setup: Products must have at least 1 supplier set in the "Purchase" tab.

🩹 Support & FAQ

🗿
"No sales history found" error?

Check if your Sales Orders are confirmed. The AI ignores Quotations and Canceled orders. Verify products are "Storable."

🔑
Where do I find my License Key?

Login to your dashboard at niyulabs.com. Your key is located under the "Active Modules" section.

🔃
A forecast is already running...

The system uses a concurrency guard. Only one sync can run at once. If stuck for >15 mins, it will auto-clear.

🛒
PO button missing?

Ensure the product has a Vendor assigned in the Purchase tab. AI cannot suggest a buy without a source.
