Inventory FAQ

Does it work with customized Odoo modules?

Yes. Niyu looks at standard stock move records. As long as your customizations generate stock moves, Niyu can forecast them.

Is my sales data safe?

We use stateless processing. We calculate velocity in volatile memory and purge it immediately. We never store your transaction history.

Can I set manual overrides?

Absolutely. You can lock specific SKUs to follow your own rules while Niyu manages the rest of your catalog.
